### should_fix
 * A move inside the village's own map cell is predicted wrongly. syncFields returns early when c.plots[0] === ci, so the field level is not lowered. groundAt always assumes fieldLv-1 for a move. Shown on w33_7000 (field level 1, soil 0.57): a move of about 1 to 2 km inside the same 5 km cell shows 'Rich ... about half as much again' (share 1.654). In play the level stayed at 1 and the site factor was 1.272, against 1.2 in the line, so the true share is 1.754, which the bands would call 'about twice'. Fix: for a move, lvNew = (c.plots && c.plots.length && c.plots[0] !== cellAt(x, y)) ? max(0, fieldLv - 1) : fieldLv, which is what syncFields does.
 * For a new village the field level is not always 0. fieldLv and plots are never reset. A clan driven out as wanderers (soc.js about line 5617: toSettle stays false when wander is true) keeps its old fieldLv and plots. When it then builds with 'Build a farming village', syncFields gives it fieldLv-1, not 0. My test on y110_s2.sav, unsettled with fieldLv 2: the line assumed quality 1.2, and the first harvest at the new village had fieldLv 1 and site factor 1.272. The brief said '0 for a new village', but also 'read syncFields and fieldLv to get this right'. The same plots condition as above would cover it. The case is rare and the error is about 6 percent.
 * The foundvillage result card has no ground line, though its map pick shows one. The brief's point 3 ('a new farming village') can be read to include a daughter village. Either add the line or confirm the narrower reading with the owner.
 * The report says new villages are compared with 'the richest known land'. richestNitF scans every land cell, known or not. In practice it is always clamped to 1.2, so it makes no difference, but the code or the report should agree.
 * Desktop only (820 px and wider): the bar is still centred on the whole window, and the longer hover line widens it over the side panel. At 1200 px it spans 301 to 899 px; the panel starts at 780. Before, it spanned 399 to 801, which also overlapped a little. This does not affect the iPhone.
 * Edge case: an unsettled clan at stage 2 or more can still have c.live 'fishing', for example after joining another tribe (soc.js about line 1013). groundAt then returns null during the settle pick, so the bar shows 'Fit ground for a village.' The result card does show the ground line, because the deed sets c.live to 'farm' first. I found this by reading the code; I did not reproduce it with the player's clan.
